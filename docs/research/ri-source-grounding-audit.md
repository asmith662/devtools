## What the original science actually asked

[Source grounding](../../experiments/source_grounding/README.md) asks whether a
source's own content materially supports an authored obligation, and whether
that judgment discriminates useful direct structural candidates. It does not
authenticate edit authority or solve unrestricted task decomposition.

`cases.py` distinguishes original0020/0021 from their replacement population:
0022 is the unchanged import-declaration task;0023 concerns direct module-body
exact function names;0024 concerns an explicit provider CPU-offload contract.
Each has two independently authored InformationNeeds and declared exclusions.
Query authorship is not an exhaustive-obligation oracle. No child, holdout or
reserve label enters the selectors.

`capture.py` constructs retained frames, canonical lexical indices, module
interpretations and native References before acquisition. A is the complete
positive canonical BM25 population with filename weight0.25. BROAD only adds
support annotations to targets already in A, from an earlier-ranked source in
the same obligation's A. Native source/target, declaration, occurrence and
derivation identities are retained; the original predecessor remains unchanged.
No rank moves, new graph-only candidates, PPR or RRF occur in this treatment.

The Sol primary sees only the obligation and own source, without query, target,
rank, structural supports or gold. Its three-way grounding judgment filters
BROAD supports into QUALIFIED, retaining uncertain potential separately.
Exact quote/overlapping-occurrence locators are resolved by the host; those
mechanics establish a source anchor, not truthful semantic usefulness.

The original global Astra gold design sees the complete parent corpus and task,
without treatment results. Necessary-unit alternatives and minimal witness unions
are deterministic consequences of its semantic decisions, not independent
adjudication. `REQUIRED` means participation in a minimal witness, not necessarily
indispensability or unconditional task satisfaction. Explicit task/repository
gaps, task-backed empty alternatives and expansion bounds remain qualified.
Do not transplant empty alternatives into production `WitnessSet` semantics.

## Completion-proof consumer and scientific assurance

The real producer is `source_grounding.capture.execute`; the real consumer is
`capture.sealed`, also used by adoption authentication. This is the existing
`experiment-completion-proof-v1`, not a new supported schema. Before publishing
the proof, the capture owner hashes protocol, exact schedule, native archive,
canonical outputs, implementation and verifier; its callback checks native
journal reconstruction, aliases/types/order, correspondence, frame/runtime and
scientific construction. Successful callback bytes are bound into the proof.

Normal later verification authenticates those bytes against an **external
authoritative proof digest** and exact input bindings. It need not decode old
native archives. A fabricated caller callback returning PASS is not independently
trustworthy; source/receipt/protocol anchors and the actual producer matter.
Neither proof nor checksum proves semantic truth, fresh approval, current disk
state, rollback of external effects or engineering publication.

Original191/196-line packet builders and606/622-line codecs were inspected as
distinct implementations. The newer designation branch permits Luna/Medium only
for explicitly synthetic cases; real science remains Astra/High or Sol/High as
appropriate. AST specialization removes the other branch's fields and vocabulary.
Same-primary self-review and deterministic materialization are clearly different
from independent semantic adjudication. Unicode quote offsets are codepoints;
native AST source ranges use one-based physical lines and zero-based UTF-8
exclusive columns. The historical `SourceSpans` defect is not silently fixed by
the current production extractor or applicable to every quote codec.
