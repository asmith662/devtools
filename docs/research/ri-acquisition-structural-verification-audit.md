## Obligation architecture: recommendation remains unapproved

The [decision preparation](task-obligation-coverage-decision.md) still recommends
**A: trusted reviewed clause/obligation coverage plus bounded C: reviewed revisions**.
This checkpoint clarifies, rather than approves, that design:

- Accountability is relative to the reviewed expected clause roster. Neither
  roster coverage nor scoped readiness proves exhaustive interpretation of the
  original objective; Case0011's omitted semantic requirements remain the reason.
- Trusted caller input enables the first deterministic application composition.
  It is not a permanent requirement for a person to hand-draft every future task.
- A distinct follow-on milestone must allow agent-proposed obligations and
  independently authorized semantic review against the original objective,
  including omissions, ambiguity, contradictions, budgets and failure evidence.
  Reviewer authority may be delegated within an explicit envelope. Worker
  self-approval is not authority, and another model call alone proves no independence.
- Task-only requirements use actual review/execution receipts. Do not fabricate
  repository witnesses, relax nonempty `WitnessSet`, or treat missing source as
  non-applicability. Semantic readiness, code-check PASS, currentness, review and
  publication remain separate claims.

Pending15bae0a6, with the recorded clarification, is sufficient for the bounded
consumer design; no new planner or second obligation identity hierarchy is needed.
After maintainer decision, its independent vertical fixture must expose an omitted
mandatory clause before semantic completion, distinguish absent need/unretrieved/
unsupported/partial/contradicted evidence, and then trace corrected trusted inputs
through native acquisition, exact read, admitted action, `check_changes` PASS and
host currentness. Scripted success proves mechanism correctness, not autonomous
task decomposition. Separate genuine model evaluation would require authorization.
