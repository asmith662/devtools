# Blind-gold C-v2: A1 / A2 / global consolidation

These experiment-owned mechanics implement the prospective C-v2 method. Only a
separately authenticated Stage A and plan bind a scientific run; this document
does not approve or execute it. Old blocked runs and journals remain
immutable. No treatment judgment informs this method. Final gold definitions are
preserved; the acquisition procedure is amended without one-shot equivalence.

## Exhaustive classification

`classification` owns state-only A1: every complete parent resource is reviewed
for every frozen obligation. USEFUL means content materially bears on satisfying
or reasoning about the obligation; NO_USEFUL means none was identified after
reviewing complete content; UNCERTAIN preserves inability to determine usefulness.
A1 determines no necessity, exact evidence, units, alternatives or witnesses.
Its resource-oriented schema has only source keys and obligation/state judgments.
It contains no rationale, quote, confidence, evidence ID or decorative provenance.

Cartesian validation rejects missing/duplicate/foreign resources, obligations and
states. Absence never means NO_USEFUL. Exact input, explicit state/identity,
model/runtime/invocation records and complete coverage form the scientific record;
lack of prose is not lack of review. Coverage binds packet and response digests;
a mutable grid cannot contradict its canonical capture.

## Selective exact evidence

`packing.partition(packet, coverage)` selects only A1 USEFUL/UNCERTAIN cells.
The packing unit is a resource: complete content once with all eligible obligation
contracts and A1 states. Canonical resource/frozen obligation order determines
membership. The entire partition is hashed before any A2 entry, independently of
treatment or observed A2 output. Greedy packing observes both input/output bounds.

A2 returns EVIDENCE_FOUND or REMAINS_UNCERTAIN, semantic_statement <=512 Unicode
characters, <=4 locators, <=4096 aggregate quoted characters, uncertainty_note
<=256, completeness and bounded same-primary self-review. A1 USEFUL requires
complete EVIDENCE_FOUND with valid own-source evidence, never a silent downgrade.
A1 UNCERTAIN may resolve to evidence or remain uncertain. Inadequate representation
fails EVIDENCE_REPRESENTATION_LIMIT; it is not negative evidence.

Source-key/quote/occurrence resolution derives canonical identity and Unicode
offsets without normalization, guessing or quote repair. `archive.assemble` retains
exact evidence, full A1 matrix, invocation/response/runtime/packet provenance.
`reattach` rejects foreign and cross-cell references. Trusted callers must
authenticate invocation success and exact frozen batch membership independently;
model assertions do not establish those facts.

## Global consolidation

Only A2-reviewed cells become EvidenceCards. Internal `state` means A1 state,
`resolution_state` means A2 resolution, and `summary` is A2's semantic statement.
Cards contain mechanical/evidence IDs, uncertainty and completeness, never raw
quotes/resources or canonical document/content IDs. Explicit NO_USEFUL cells stay
in the complete host matrix, not individual cards.

`disclosure.from_archive` includes task/obligations, coverage root, total resource/
cell counts, NO_USEFUL counts by obligation, every eligible card and unresolved
IDs. Literal Unicode length framing permits exact replay without JSON escape
multiplication or truncation. Worst-case sizing assumes every A1 cell USEFUL,
maximum statement/uncertainty lengths and four evidence IDs per card.

Global Astra/High determines necessary units, conjunctions, alternatives, gaps,
sufficiency, qualifications and self-review through exact cell/evidence references.
`consolidation.assemble` reattaches archived spans and uses existing minimal-union
semantics and million-combination safeguards. REQUIRED is participation in an
accepted minimal witness; other evidence-bearing cells are HELPFUL_ONLY. Only
explicit A1 NO_USEFUL with complete coverage and no contradiction becomes
UNNECESSARY. Its canonical reason records the explicit A1 state and coverage root,
not an invented model rationale. Remaining uncertainty fails canonical assembly
rather than being relabeled. Canonical review validation and builders remain
independently mandatory.

## Capacity, spend and authority

Every host-valid semantic response has a constructive maximum <=65536 canonical
UTF-8 bytes. Native schema validity cannot enforce an aggregate quote-string sum;
the host enforces that frozen cross-field assertion. Candidate input sizing allows
<=700000 Unicode characters AND <=700000 UTF-8 bytes, bound to the compatible
large-input CapacityReceipt. A separate OutputCapacityReceipt demonstrates 63357
canonical output bytes, exceeding the largest gold response maximum (61326).
Actual global input must pass both before semantic entry;
the method does not promise completion for every possible upstream finding.

`execution` and `lifecycle` bind the existing `semantic_limits` mechanism to the
frozen partition, exact pre-entry input/schema gates and durable reservations.
A1 requires 3/3/2 calls for 0022/0023/0024. Each case reserves at most 24 A2 calls
and one global call. Together these imply at most 72 A2, 83 fresh Astra and,
including fresh 0024 treatment, 84 fresh semantic calls. The per-case maximum is
28 including treatment. The complete dynamic A2 partition is reserved together;
an excessive partition stops the whole A2 stage before its first entry, without
sampling, dropping resources, repacking or changing evidence. These are resource
governance limits, never relevance or label thresholds. Original adopted Sol
invocations are not fresh calls or duplicated usage. Existing approval and dead
journal checks remain mandatory: budget availability is not authority.

`execution.validate` replays exact partitions, coverage, archive, structured
responses, authorized disclosure bytes, raw process/usage captures and canonical
artifacts. The standalone canonical builder remains mandatory. Scientific capture
and usage files travel with the primary artifact closure and survive import
without transformation. The 128 MiB expanded host capture bound also fails closed.
No implementation claims every possible upstream result can complete downstream.

`fresh` preserves the original Sol instructions, packet, verdicts and locator
schema. Its preflight callback runs before the native grounding ENTERED marker;
its accepted canonical response has a host output limit of 63357 bytes. The
original unbounded native schema is retained to preserve treatment semantics;
oversized actual output fails rather than being truncated. CLI transport/tool
compatibility is assumed model independent; Astra receipts do not establish Sol
semantic quality or an independently known model context size.

`prospective` reproduces the existing public tasks, parent frames and authoring
exactly, without child access. Adopted seals remain unchanged. A separately
bound compatibility statement documents the optional preflight callback and
original verifier-source attribution; an exact AST comparison proves those are
the only capture changes. Original completed branches retain original proofs,
usage, runtime and implementation identities, without retroactive publication.
Six retained old journals are exact path/hash exceptions to clean-worktree
authentication, with durable exact copies in the new Stage A. No other dirty
worktree exception is granted and the originals remain untouched.

A1 is the raw-usefulness selection bottleneck. A2 cannot recover resources A1
labeled NO_USEFUL; global consolidation cannot recover evidence A2 omitted.
Multiple fresh Astra calls increase burden and independent failure opportunities.
No second reviewer, reconciliation or automatic outer retry is introduced.
Built-in provider transport behavior is backend-managed; underlying request count
is UNKNOWN. Mandatory usage acceptance is separate from transient display telemetry.
Adoption retains original invocation/usage exactly once. Synthetic validation and
engineering receipts supply no scientific result or execution authority.
