Case 0018 is STRUCTURAL_DISCRIMINATION_SIGNAL (one strong beneficial, zero strong harmful); Cases 0017 and 0019 have NO_MATERIAL_STRUCTURAL_DISCRIMINATION_SIGNAL. Among 121 pairs, 7 are beneficial, 110 neutral and 4 harmful. Direct support reaches 6/39 REQUIRED-in-A cells and 15/121 supported targets are useful; 106/121 are UNNECESSARY. The cohort supported-useful rate (12.3967%) exceeds baseline A's descriptive 9.2308%, but this is neither causal improvement nor consistent cross-case evidence: Case 0018 has only 8/96 useful supported targets against 72/904 baseline useful candidates. The single strong signal and absence of strong harm keep the bounded evidence question open; high neutrality/noise and low required coverage do not justify consuming this broad condition as a promotion policy. This interpretation creates no cohort gate, changes no case category, authorizes no production behavior and validates no reranker. The maintainer decides the next architecture.
