# Separate treatment-blind Stage C.5 protocol — PREPARED ONLY

Reviewed gold is frozen before packet construction. The packet purpose is to
independently determine whether the manually frozen InformationNeeds semantically
cover reviewed REQUIRED information units. No C.5 adjudication occurs in this
checkpoint. Stage D is not performed and U1 effectiveness remains UNKNOWN.

Only original task, nine frozen obligations/criteria, pre-acquisition need
identities/statements/reasons/task anchors/author provenance, neutral required-unit
identities/statements, alternative membership needed for complementarity, neutral
instructions and integrity identities are supplied. Gold answer locations,
filenames, excerpts, queries/terms/routes, results/candidates/ranks/scores/costs,
arms/outcomes, source adjudication identities/chronology/models and confirmation
are excluded. Answer-location mentions inside three required statements are
projected to semantic descriptions; exact original statements remain in reviewed
gold. The reviewer receives neither the projection audit nor repository checkout.

The full mapping frame is every need × every required unit, including all
cross-obligation pairs. No lexical overlap pruning is permitted. All members of
one witness alternative are complementary; any complete alternative suffices.
Incompatible alternatives may not be mixed. REQUIRED means necessary in at least
one acceptable alternative, not in every valid task solution.

Mapping labels:

- DIRECTLY_COVERS: explicitly seeks evidence capable of establishing the unit.
- PARTIALLY_COVERS: seeks only part and would not reasonably establish it fully.
- DOES_NOT_COVER: asks a materially different repository question.
- AMBIGUOUS: relationship cannot be defended confidently from the packet.

Every direct mapping explains the fact sought, the fact established, and why an
answer would establish the unit. Shared wording alone is insufficient.

Unit coverage: COVERED has at least one direct mapping; PARTIAL_ONLY has no direct
and at least one partial; AMBIGUOUS_ONLY has no direct/partial and at least one
ambiguous; UNCOVERED has no direct/partial/ambiguous. AMBIGUOUS_ONLY takes precedence
over UNCOVERED to keep these categories disjoint. U1 requires DIRECT coverage.

Need classifications are supplied by the future semantic reviewer with rationale:
NECESSARY directly covers at least one unit no other need directly covers;
USEFUL_REDUNDANT directly covers units all covered by other needs; PARTIAL_ONLY
has partial mappings but no direct; UNNECESSARY has neither direct nor partial;
MISFORMULATED has recognizable intended concern but cannot reasonably guide
acquisition of the fact; AMBIGUOUS cannot be defensibly classified. MISFORMULATED
and AMBIGUOUS are reviewer judgments, not forced word-overlap/count categories.

The exact frozen definitions are in adjudication/stage_c5/packet.json.gz and
C5_INSTRUCTIONS.md. C5_PACKET_REVIEW.md provides repository-side manual transparency,
not C.5 gold. Manifest binds reviewed_gold_sha256 and frozen_needs_sha256; explicit
archive_sha256, canonical_payload_sha256, manifest_sha256 and integrity_sha256
scopes are documented in the reviewed publication. Deterministic double-build,
complete identity frame, strict metadata allowlists, leakage rejection, sterile
four-file whitelist and overwrite refusal must pass before future review starts.

The prepared workspace is external, contains no Git/checkout/query/result
artifacts, and has no links/junctions/reparse points. The future reviewer freezes
all semantic mappings, rationales, need classifications, coverage derivation and
digests before any later treatment join. No such decisions exist yet.
