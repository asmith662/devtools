# Case 0012 frozen Stage C reliability comparison protocol

FROZEN BEFORE C-R; NOT EXECUTED

## Boundary and freeze

This protocol is repository-side only, frozen before independent C-R exists. Do not execute comparison now. Neither adjudication is truth. No reviewer receives this protocol, PRIMARY structures, known tensions, treatment data or comparison expectations. Criteria may not be tuned after C-R is seen. Any future amendment must preserve this version and explain its timing and effect.

## Authentication and comparability

Authenticate both sealed publications, physical hashes, deterministic replay and explicit blindness attestations before comparison. Require identical case, task text/identity, native frame identities, qualified resource identities/content and all nine obligation statements/criteria/task bases. Compare applicability explicitly. Missing or duplicate cells, identity/frame mismatch, absent required attestation or failed authentication is NON_COMPARABLE: no reliability outcome; Stage D remains blocked. Preserve independent artifacts unchanged.

## Qualified cells and exact metrics

Compare all 4,779 cells keyed by obligation identity plus resource address, content identity and document identity. Report the full four-by-four label confusion matrix, overall exact agreement, and each obligation's matrix/agreement. For REQUIRED and HELPFUL_ONLY separately report qualified-cell intersections, unions, differences and Jaccard. Empty/empty Jaccard is 1; one empty and one nonempty is 0. Report resource-level required unions and their intersections/unions/differences/Jaccard separately; never collapse cell and resource scopes. Preserve every UNRESOLVED judgment.

## Neutral unit alignment

Do not equate independently authored unit IDs or wording. First propose neutral semantic propositions using original task clauses, obligation criterion, scope, necessity, admissible inference and packet evidence. Map each review independently onto those propositions with provenance; permit one-to-many and many-to-one alignment. Record granularity differences, added/omitted requirements and uncertain alignments. No majority, PRIMARY preference or C-R preference establishes truth. Uncertain alignment cannot establish HIGH_RELIABILITY. Alignment may compare meanings but cannot rewrite either sealed judgment.

## Witnesses and sufficiency

For each obligation compare unit sets/granularity, supporting qualified cells, explicit/inferable/task-backed evidence, complementary all-of members, substitutable alternatives, and obligation-indispensable resource/unit intersections. Enumerate complete cross-obligation combinations and compare distinct sufficient resource unions/ranges, aligned unit unions/ranges and task-indispensable resources/units. Report raw structures and semantically aligned structures. Compare proofs after neutral alignment: differing IDs/order or equivalent support multiplicity alone is not a necessity disagreement, but differing admissible sufficient resource sets, necessary facts, evidence/inference conditions or indispensability is material. Preserve empty resource sets and ambiguity; do not assume independently authored unit counts must match.

## Gaps and limitations

Compare task gaps, repository-information gaps, interpretation limitations, preserved API/cardinality ambiguities and unresolved resource judgments. Distinguish wording-only variation from different implementation constraints, supported scope, missing information or admissibility. An uncertain distinction is recorded unresolved; it cannot be silently reconciled or classified HIGH_RELIABILITY.

## Route-family relevance without treatment access

Determine route-family-relevant target necessity only from the original task's explicit target/anchor categories and the blind packet's addressed resources. Do not open actual hints, routes, queries, rankings, arms, acquisition costs or treatment outputs to decide reliability. All qualified REQUIRED disagreements trigger the severe gate regardless of whether a target is ultimately route-family relevant.

## Outcome precedence and objective criteria

Apply authentication/comparability first, then SEVERE_ARCHITECTURE_RELEVANT_DISAGREEMENT, then HIGH_RELIABILITY, otherwise MATERIAL_DISAGREEMENT. Thresholds are fixed here before C-R. No aggregate agreement can override the semantic gates.

## SEVERE_ARCHITECTURE_RELEVANT_DISAGREEMENT

Classify SEVERE if any qualified cell differs in REQUIRED membership (including route-family targets), any task or repository-information gap differs substantively, any aligned necessary unit is added/omitted, any complete sufficient resource alternative/union differs, any witness admission/inference condition or indispensable resource/aligned fact differs, or an interpretation limitation changes implementation feasibility/ownership/admissibility. An applicability change that changes necessity or sufficiency also triggers SEVERE. Stage D remains blocked pending treatment-blind reconciliation.

## HIGH_RELIABILITY

Require all of: no severe trigger; identical applicability; no unresolved cells or uncertain semantic alignments; equal aligned necessity, witness/sufficiency and indispensability semantics; equal substantive gaps and constraints; REQUIRED qualified-cell Jaccard = 1; overall exact cell-label agreement >= 0.98; every obligation's exact agreement >= 0.95; HELPFUL_ONLY qualified-cell Jaccard >= 0.90. Minor helpful/unnecessary label differences and purely representational granularity differences may remain if all conditions pass; report them explicitly. HIGH_RELIABILITY alone does not authorize Stage D or designate either reviewer as truth; reviewed/reconciled publication and separate Stage D authorization remain required.

## MATERIAL_DISAGREEMENT

Classify MATERIAL when comparable reviews have no severe trigger but fail any HIGH condition: threshold shortfall, unresolved judgment/alignment, applicability difference without a severe effect, or substantive limitation difference without a severe effect. Stage D remains blocked until treatment-blind review/reconciliation resolves relevant uncertainty. Do not choose a winner from agreement metrics.

Publish authenticated inputs and hashes, case/task/frame bindings, metrics and cell differences, neutral alignments with provenance/uncertainty, raw and aligned witness/combination comparisons, ranges/unions/indispensability, gaps/limitations, outcome with exact triggering criteria, and remaining blocks. A disagreement packet may be created only after C-R is sealed and comparison is separately authorized. No effectiveness claim or treatment join belongs to this protocol.
